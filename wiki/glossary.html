Glossary

Domain terms, key dates, and the theory behind the dialogue model.

Project & period

TermMeaning
Project Cybersyn (Synco)Stafford Beer's cybernetic system for managing Chile's nationalized economy in real time under Allende (1971–1973). Centred on the Operations Room (Opsroom). The project's namesake and aesthetic.
Allende eraThe Unidad Popular government of Salvador Allende, 1970–1973. The project window is Oct 1969 – Sep 1973.
Stafford BeerBritish cyberneticist who designed Cybersyn; source of the Viable System Model.
CORFOChilean production-development corporation; central to nationalized industry (category 4).

Speech-act & commitment theory

The dialogue model (in schemas) draws on Searle's speech acts and the Flores–Winograd "conversation for action" / commitment cycle:

ConceptValues
SpeechAct (Searle 1969)Assertive, Directive, Commissive, Expressive, Declarative.
CommitmentStage (Flores–Winograd 1986)Preparation → Negotiation → Performance → Acceptance → Closed-Satisfied / Closed-Broken.
CommitmentActionRequest, Offer, Promise, Counter-offer, Decline, Cancel, Declare-complete, Declare-satisfied, Withdraw.
Timeline kind groups (Cast Manager)The four narrative groups a character timeline can cover — acciones (event), enunciados (statement, qualified by a SpeechAct), información (role / affiliation / other) and rumores (rumor: an unverified assertion, carrying reported_by and a low confidence). Diversity across groups feeds the strict completeness metric (Cast Manager).
