The 16×13×48 Coverage Matrix

The project tracks 16 thematic categories × 13 genres × 48 months (Oct 1969 – Sep 1973) = 9,984 cells. The pipeline's job is to fill every plausible cell with enough verified period sources — the genre axis is what gives NPC dialogue its diversity of registers.

▶ Genre axis — built (June 2026)

The matrix was originally 16×48 (theme × month); the genre axis made it 3D. All layers are migrated: registry (lib/genres.py), shared coverage table (lib/coverage.py), Mapper genre classification, Propositor 3D ranking, and the Admin theme×genre heatmap. Follow-ups: re-map the existing corpus so it gains genres (make remap-all; until then old docs sit in genre 0), and curate plausibility priors as the corpus and the Cambridge key-gated APIs teach us what exists.

Why genre?

The simulation's end goal is LLM-driven NPC dialogue grounded in period sources. A matrix cell's genre determines the speech register a document contributes: a decree teaches performative legal language, a telex teaches interpersonal speech acts (Flores's own theory), a poem teaches lyric idiom. Steering research by genre guarantees register diversity per theme, which months alone never did. Months remain as document metadata and mission-priority boosts (critical months).

Plausibility weights — not all cells are real

The month axis was uniform; the genre axis is not (Deporte × Cable diplomático is structurally near-empty). Each genre carries a default prior plus per-theme overrides (Genre.plausibility(category_id), 0–1). The Propositor multiplies cell priority by this prior, so implausible cells sink to the bottom of the queue without being hard-excluded, and weighted coverage percentages use the prior as denominator weight. Priors are curatable data — adjust them in lib/genres.py as the corpus teaches us what exists.

How a cell is scored

coverage_score = min(1.0, docs_found / 5) — 5 verified documents in a (theme, genre, month) cell counts as 100% coverage. The Propositor ranks cell gaps (× plausibility) and generates missions; the Archivero fills them; the Verificator approves them; the Mapper assigns each verified document to a precise cell — theme, genre and month. The Admin heatmap shows 2D projections of the cube (theme × month, theme × genre).

Critical months (Z axis)

Months are the third axis: documents keep their mapped_month_iso, and _CRITICAL_MONTHS in the Propositor boosts priority for key events — e.g. 1972-10 (paro de camioneros / trucker strike) and 1973-09 (the coup). See Glossary for the full timeline.
