Project Cybersyn — Research Pipeline

A multi-agent research pipeline for a game-based simulation of Project Cybersyn (Allende-era Chile, 1969–1973), built for an MPhil in Digital Humanities at Cambridge. The pipeline harvests historical documents, structures them into a 16×13×48 (theme × genre × month) coverage matrix, and will eventually power LLM-driven NPC dialogue grounded in period sources.

▶ Working Protocol — read this first

This wiki is the ultimate source of truth and the rules of the project.

  • Before any work: read Rules, then the wiki page(s) covering the area you are about to touch.
  • After work is done: update the affected wiki page(s) so the wiki never lags the code. A change isn't finished until the wiki reflects it.
  • Commit after every relevant change (code + its wiki update, same commit) and push — the remote is the backup. See Rules › Git.
  • CLAUDE.md is intentionally a thin pointer to this wiki — the detail lives here, in one place, to avoid drift.
